My research interests lie at the intersection of optimization and machine learning. I am particularly interested in designing and analyzing first-order methods that adapt to the geometry and stochastic structure of modern learning problems.

Optimization for machine learning
======

I am interested in optimization algorithms that are computationally efficient, theoretically grounded, and effective for large-scale machine-learning models.

Adaptive and preconditioned methods
======

My interests include adaptive gradient and preconditioned first-order methods, especially how scaling, curvature information, and matrix structure can improve optimization.

Stochastic optimization
======

I am interested in the behavior of optimization algorithms under noisy or sampled gradients, including convergence, stability, and practical performance.
