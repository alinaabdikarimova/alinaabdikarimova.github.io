I am an M.S. student in Applied Mathematics at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST). My research interests lie at the intersection of optimization and machine learning, with a particular focus on adaptive and preconditioned first-order methods.

I am interested in developing principled, efficient optimization methods and understanding how algorithm design, geometry, and stochasticity interact in modern machine-learning problems.

Research interests
======

- Optimization for machine learning
- Adaptive gradient methods
- Preconditioned first-order methods
- Stochastic optimization
- Matrix optimization
